What gets checked
  • Flight duty limit (Table 2 and extension table), rest before each flight, a night's sleep after a late finish before an early start, standby 16h, duty hours in 7, 14 and 28 days, and a 36-hour break every 7 days.
  • From the PDF: report time, debrief minus 30 minutes as on-blocks, routes, layovers. Sectors marked * are positioning and don't count. Days off, rest days and annual leave come in separately. Rest days and leave are locked, so they can't be put in a swap.
  • Night means 22:00–08:00, early start is a report 05:00–05:59, late finish is 23:00–01:59 (standard EASA meanings).
  • Not checked: flight hours, acclimatisation, hotel travel time, section 7.1.7.
